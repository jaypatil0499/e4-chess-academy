Our curriculum

Three levels. One path.

Each programme has a clear progression route — a child can enter at any level and keep climbing.

♙ Beginner

Pawn to Player

First moves · Ages 5+
  • Board setup, piece names and movement
  • Legal moves, check and checkmate basics
  • Simple tactics: fork, pin, skewer
  • Introduction to openings (e4, d4)
  • Fun mini-tournaments every week
  • Monthly parent progress reports
♞ Intermediate

Knight's Journey

Competitive footing
  • Advanced tactics: discovered attack, deflection
  • Endgame mastery: king and pawn endings
  • Opening theory: Ruy López, Sicilian
  • Strategic thinking and pawn structure
  • FIDE-rated tournament preparation
  • Bi-weekly one-on-one coaching
♛ Advanced

Champion's Path

Rated play · Titled coaches
  • Deep opening repertoire and novelties
  • Complex middle-game plans and calculation
  • Endgame theory: rook, bishop, knight
  • Psychological preparation for tournaments
  • National and international rating development
  • Weekly sessions with titled coaches
